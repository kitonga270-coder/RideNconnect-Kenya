Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ride on RideConnect Kenya?

Click "Book a Ride", enter your pickup and destination, choose your service type, compare available drivers and fares, then confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation via WhatsApp.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept M-Pesa (Paybill 100400, Account 9783823235), cash payments directly to drivers, and card payments. Corporate clients get monthly invoicing.

How do I become a driver on RideConnect?

Visit our Drivers page, fill out the registration form, submit your documents (National ID, driving license, vehicle logbook, certificate of good conduct), and we'll verify and onboard you within 48 hours.

Is RideConnect available 24 hours?

Yes! We operate 24/7. Our airport transfer and taxi services are available round the clock. Matatu routes follow their standard operating hours.

How do I track my ride?

After booking, you'll receive a WhatsApp message with your driver's details and a tracking link. You can track in real-time until pickup.

Do you offer promo codes and discounts?

Yes! New users get 20% off their first 3 rides. Refer a friend and earn KES 100 bonus each. Follow us on social media @Hitman_jojo for exclusive promo codes.
