Nakuru's #1 Transport Platform

We Move Nakuru Forward

RideConnect Kenya was born from a simple observation: getting around Nakuru City was unnecessarily complicated. Passengers struggled to find reliable rides, and good drivers had no platform to reach customers efficiently.

We built RideConnect Kenya to solve both problems. Our platform connects passengers instantly with verified Uber drivers, matatu operators, taxi drivers, and private car owners — all in one place.

Based in Nakuru City, we understand the unique transport needs of our community. From daily commutes to airport transfers, corporate travel to parcel delivery — we've got Nakuru covered.
